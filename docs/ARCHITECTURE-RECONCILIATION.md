# Architecture Reconciliation: Workshop Decisions (#1–#188) vs. Prior Architecture

## 1. Document Context and Governance Notice

This document establishes the official reconciliation between the prior architecture documentation (Phase 01–06: ADRs, DDRs, Candidate Domain Models, and Schema Readiness) and the business-policy decisions formulated in the manual **Decision Workshop (Decisions #1 through #188)** for the **Sistem Informasi Pelaporan Insiden Keselamatan Pasien (IKP) IBS RSUD Prof. Dr. W. Z. Johannes Kupang**.

**CRITICAL GOVERNANCE MANDATE:**
The manual Decision Workshop (#1–#188) provides concrete, authoritative **business-policy design inputs** that guide the physical implementation of Phase 07. However, this workshop does **NOT** substitute for formal hospital decree or statutory governance approval. Historical decisions from Phase 01–06 are retained for auditability and marked with explicit reconciliation statuses.

---

## 2. Reconciliation Status Taxonomy

- **`ACTIVE`:** Decision is active, in force, and directly implemented in Phase 07 production foundation.
- **`SUPERSEDED`:** Previous architectural recommendation is formally replaced by a workshop business policy decision. The previous decision is retained in historical records for traceability.
- **`CONFLICTING`:** Material divergence between baseline documents requiring explicit reconciliation handling.
- **`REQUIRES FORMAL APPROVAL`:** Decision carries statutory or clinical governance weight that must receive formal institutional sign-off prior to external audit.
- **`TECHNICAL DEFAULT`:** Engineering choice adopted to fulfill workshop intent while maintaining reversibility and simplicity without requiring separate business debate.
