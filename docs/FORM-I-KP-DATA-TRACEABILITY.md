# Form IKP Data Traceability Matrix

## 1. Document Purpose and Status

This document establishes the bidirectional traceability between the physical official reporting form **"Form IKP.pdf"**, the product requirements in **"AI-Product-Blueprint-Sistem-Informasi-IKP-IBS-RSUD-WZ-Johannes.md"**, and the candidate domain data model for the **Sistem Informasi Pelaporan Insiden Keselamatan Pasien (IKP) IBS RSUD Prof. Dr. W. Z. Johannes Kupang**.

**STATUS: PROVISIONAL / CANDIDATE DATA TRACEABILITY — PENDING STAKEHOLDER FORM VERIFICATION.**

_Note on Source Verification:_ The text of `docs/Form IKP.pdf` could not be machine-extracted during earlier repository audits because it is a scanned image document. Field definitions below are derived from Section 3 of the Product Blueprint (`docs/AI-Product-Blueprint-*.md:28-34`), cross-referenced with National Patient Safety Committee (KNKP) hospital accreditation standards. A manual field-by-field verification with hospital clinical governance remains an open prerequisite (`DM-024`).

---

## 2. Source Classification Legend

- **Directly supported by Form IKP:** Explicitly present in the physical IKP reporting form layout.
- **Supported by Blueprint:** Defined in the Product Blueprint's Functional Requirements, Business Rules, or Data Dictionary.
- **Technical Derived Field:** Field required by technical architecture, auditing, concurrency control, or edge serverless data integrity.
- **Unresolved / Decision Pending:** Field presence, format, or nullability depends on an unapproved stakeholder decision.
