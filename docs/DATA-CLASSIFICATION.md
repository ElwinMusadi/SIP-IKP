# Data Classification and Protection Policy

## 1. Scope and Legal Baseline

This document classifies all data categories handled by the **Sistem Informasi Pelaporan Insiden Keselamatan Pasien (IKP) IBS RSUD Prof. Dr. W. Z. Johannes Kupang**.

**LEGAL NOTICE:** Data classifications herein represent an internal technical and operational privacy framework derived from the Product Blueprint's strict confidentiality requirement (_"RAHASIA, TIDAK BOLEH DIFOTOCOPY"_, `BR-02`), standard hospital data protection practices, and medical record confidentiality baselines. This document does not claim external legal certification or statutory privacy compliance until audited by authorized hospital counsel.

## 3. Spreadsheet Formula Injection Defense

When exporting patient or clinical incident data to CSV or Excel (`FR-07`), fields containing user input (e.g. `incident_title`, `chronology_narrative`, `immediate_action_and_result`, `patient_name`, `overdue_reason`, `recommendation_text`, `action_text`) could be maliciously crafted to trigger formula execution when opened in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

**Candidate Protection Rule (`PROVISIONAL`):**
Any string field exported to CSV whose first character is one of:

- `=` (Equal)
- `+` (Plus)
- `-` (Minus)
- `@` (At)
- `\t` (Tab)
- `\r` (Carriage return)

must be prefixed with a single quote character (`'`) or prepended with an apostrophe to force spreadsheet engines to treat the cell contents strictly as plain text.
